Silenced Irony

Short film, 2026. Ten minutes on climate change.

Open on YouTube (click here if the above is showing unavailable)

Synopsis

As climate change intensifies, a group of students confronts its devastating consequences when a water crisis strikes their school. Tanmay, haunted by his sister Sharanya’s mysterious death while exposing an illegal water racket, finds purpose through Richa, a young innovator researching climate solutions. Together with Shaurya, they launch a city-wide campaign using technology, awareness and collective action to inspire change. As their efforts transform attitudes and bring hope, the story exposes a deeper contradiction: those who passionately advocate environmental protection often fail to practise what they preach. Ultimately, Silenced Irony questions whether humanity truly seeks change or merely performs it.

Themes

  • The water crisisA shortage at the students’ school sets the story in motion.
  • A silenced investigationTanmay carries the unanswered death of his sister, who was exposing an illegal water racket.
  • Collective actionTechnology, awareness and community effort drive a city-wide campaign.
  • Saying versus doingThe film asks whether we want change or only perform wanting it.

Faasla

Short film, 2026. A story about pottery and the distance between generations.

Open on YouTube (click here if the above is showing unavailable)

Synopsis

When Kairav, a stressed Class 12 student, struggles to find an idea for his school’s cultural heritage presentation, he unexpectedly discovers the world of pottery through Narayana, a traditional sculpture teacher. What begins as an awkward encounter between two generations, filled with misunderstandings and humorous exchanges over language and slang, gradually becomes a meaningful learning experience. As Narayana introduces Kairav to the art of pottery, both begin to understand each other’s worlds. Through humour, tradition and an unlikely bond, the story explores how differences in language and age can create a communication gap between generations. By the end, both have exchanged cultures, traditions and habits, learning something from one another.

Themes

  • The generation gapLanguage and slang cause misunderstandings, and a lot of humour, between a student and his teacher.
  • Heritage and craftPottery becomes the bridge, passed on by a traditional sculpture teacher.
  • Cultural exchangeEach of them leaves with the other’s traditions and habits.

Best Short Film

Silenced Irony at the Purple Umbrella Festival 2026

Silenced Irony won Best Short Film at the National Purple Umbrella Film Festival. It was awarded at the Dr. Ambedkar International Centre, New Delhi, on 6 October 2026, for a film about a water crisis, a silenced investigation, and the gap between what we say about the planet and what we do. Click here to see the official instagram post.

About the project

About the festival

The Purple Umbrella Festival 2026 took place at the Dr. Ambedkar International Centre in New Delhi under the theme “AI & Climate: Reimagining the Future Together”. It brought over 1,000 school students together with educators, climate experts, artists and innovators, with theatre, short films and documentaries, and student innovations on the programme. It is part of the Purple Umbrella Foundation’s mission to empower one million young climate champions.
